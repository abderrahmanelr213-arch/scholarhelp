## Short answer

**Most scholarship applications fail because of avoidable mistakes: not meeting a rule, missing a document, ignoring the question, or sending a general essay.** Read the official page twice, make a checklist of every requirement, write a specific answer for each scholarship, ask for recommendations early and submit a few days before the deadline.

## Which mistakes happen before anyone reads my essay?

1. **Not checking eligibility.** Age, nationality, degree, years of experience and language rules are strict. If you do not meet one, do not apply.
2. **Missing documents.** One missing transcript or signature can end an application. Use our [scholarship documents checklist](/guides/scholarship-application-documents-checklist/).
3. **Wrong format.** Follow the file type, size, page and word limits exactly.
4. **Applying at the last minute.** Portals crash near deadlines, and the time zone may not be yours.

## Which mistakes weaken my essay?

5. **Not answering the question.** Read the prompt and answer it in your first paragraph.
6. **A general letter.** If your letter could go to any scholarship, it is too general. Name the programme and connect it to your goals. See [how to write a motivation letter](/guides/how-to-write-a-motivation-letter/).
7. **Claims without proof.** "I am a leader" means little without an example and a result.
8. **Copying or paying for an essay.** Committees notice, and some disqualify applicants for it. Write it yourself and ask for feedback.
9. **Small errors.** Spelling mistakes and the wrong university name suggest carelessness.

## Which mistakes involve other people?

10. **Asking for recommendations too late.** Give your recommender at least two weeks and a short summary of your goals. Read our [recommendation letter tips](/guides/recommendation-letters/).
11. **A weak CV.** Keep it to one or two clear pages with results. Our [scholarship CV guide](/guides/how-to-write-a-cv-for-a-scholarship/) has a template.

## Which mistake can cost me money?

12. **Paying for a "scholarship".** Real scholarships rarely charge to apply, and none should charge to "guarantee" a place. Read our guide to [fake scholarships and paid summits](/guides/fake-scholarships-and-paid-summits/) before paying anything.

## What should I check before I press submit?

- I meet every eligibility rule, including language
- every document is uploaded, readable and correctly named
- each essay answers its question within the word limit
- my recommenders have submitted their letters
- names, dates and the programme name are correct
- I have saved a copy of everything
