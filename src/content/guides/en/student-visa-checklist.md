## Start as soon as you get the award letter

Visa appointments can take weeks in some countries. Book yours as soon as you have your **admission and scholarship letters**.

## Documents most embassies ask for

- Valid passport (usually valid for at least six months after your planned stay)
- University admission letter
- Scholarship award letter showing what it covers
- Proof of accommodation, often provided by the scholarship
- Health insurance
- Passport photos in the required size
- Academic certificates and transcripts, sometimes translated and certified

## Tips

- Read the embassy website of the country where you will study, not a forum.
- Keep paper copies and scans of everything.
- If the scholarship pays your stipend, bring its letter: it proves you can cover your living costs.
