## Short answer

**Ten funded opportunities on ScholarHelp close between 8 November and 31 December 2026.** The earliest are the UN Indigenous Peoples travel grants (8 November), then OIST and Falling Walls (15 November). Pick the ones you clearly qualify for, put their deadlines in your calendar, and start with the closest one. Dates below were checked against each official page when we listed them, so always confirm on the official link before you submit.

## Which opportunities close in November 2026?

- **[UN Voluntary Fund for Indigenous Peoples travel grants](/opportunities/un-indigenous-peoples-fund-travel-grants-2027/)**: travel and a stipend for Indigenous representatives to attend UN sessions in New York or Geneva in 2027. **Deadline: 8 November.** No test; apply in English, French or Spanish.
- **[OIST PhD Program (Japan)](/opportunities/oist-phd-program-2027-japan/)**: a fully funded five-year science PhD with tuition, a yearly stipend and housing support. **Deadline: 15 November.** TOEFL or IELTS only if your degree was not in English, with no minimum score.
- **[Falling Walls Female Science Talents (Germany)](/opportunities/falling-walls-female-science-talents-2027/)**: two funded trips to Berlin for women scientists, with flights, hotel and meals covered. **Deadline: 15 November.** Fluent English, no test.
- **[ETH Zurich Excellence Scholarship (Switzerland)](/opportunities/eth-zurich-excellence-scholarship-2027/)**: a living stipend and full tuition waiver for top master's students. **Apply 1 to 30 November.** TOEFL, IELTS or Cambridge, unless your bachelor's was fully in English.
- **[Yenching Academy, Peking University (China)](/opportunities/yenching-academy-2027-peking-university/)**: a fully funded master's in China Studies with housing, a stipend and travel. **Deadline: 30 November.** IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL 100, waived for English-taught degrees.

## Which opportunities close in December 2026?

- **[Ernst Mach Grant Worldwide (Austria)](/opportunities/ernst-mach-grant-worldwide-austria-2027/)**: a research stay at an Austrian university for PhD students and young postdocs. **Deadline: 1 December.** No test certificate needed.
- **[Japan Foundation Japanese Studies Fellowship](/opportunities/japan-foundation-japanese-studies-fellowship-2027/)**: airfare and a stipend for research in Japan. **Deadline: 1 December.** Japanese or English, no test score.
- **[Clarendon Scholarships, Oxford (UK)](/opportunities/clarendon-scholarships-oxford-2027/)**: course fees and a living grant, with no separate form. **Course deadlines from 2 December** (some in January). IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL 100, waived after a recent English-taught degree.
- **[Gates Cambridge Scholarship (UK)](/opportunities/gates-cambridge-scholarship-2027/)**: full-cost scholarships for students from outside the UK. **8 December or 6 January**, depending on your course. Often IELTS 7.5, usually waived after an English-taught degree.
- **[MBZUAI scholarships (Abu Dhabi)](/opportunities/mbzuai-graduate-scholarship-2027/)**: fully funded PhD places in artificial intelligence with a monthly stipend. **Deadline: 15 December.** IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL 90, may be waived.

Also closing on 31 December for non-Europeans: the **[EMLE Erasmus Mundus master in law and economics](/opportunities/erasmus-mundus-emle-law-economics-2027/)**. See our [Erasmus Mundus guide](/guides/how-to-apply-for-erasmus-mundus-scholarships/) for how it works.

## How do I plan six weeks of applications?

1. **This week:** choose two to four opportunities you clearly qualify for. Read each official page and list the documents.
2. **Week 2:** ask your referees, giving them the deadline and a short list of what you did with them.
3. **Weeks 3 and 4:** write one motivation letter, then adapt it for each programme. Book an English test now if you need one, since results can take two weeks.
4. **Weeks 5 and 6:** submit at least three days before each deadline, because portals get slow on the last day.

## Which ones need no English test?

If you do not have IELTS or TOEFL yet, start with Falling Walls, the Ernst Mach Grant, the Japan Foundation fellowship and the UN travel grants. Our guide to [funded opportunities without IELTS](/guides/funded-opportunities-without-ielts/) lists more.
