## Who to ask

Choose people who **know your work well**: a professor who supervised your project, or a manager who saw your results. A short, detailed letter from someone who knows you beats a vague letter from a famous name.

## When to ask

Ask at least **four weeks before the deadline**. Teachers are busy, especially near the end of the semester.

## What to send them

- The scholarship name, link and deadline.
- Your CV and your motivation letter.
- Two or three things you would like them to mention, such as a project, a grade or a skill.
- How to submit: some portals email the referee directly.

## After they submit

Send a short thank-you message and tell them the result. You may need their help again.
