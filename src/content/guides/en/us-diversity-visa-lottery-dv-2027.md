## The short answer

- **DV-2027 registration is not open yet.** As of 16 October 2026, the official website [dvprogram.state.gov](https://dvprogram.state.gov/) says entries are not being accepted.
- **A US federal court has ordered it to open.** On 14 October 2026 a federal judge in San Diego ordered the US Department of State to run the DV-2027 lottery.
- **The court deadlines** (the latest dates the State Department can act; it may act earlier):
  - Announce the registration dates and rules: by **29 October 2026**
  - Open registration: by **1 November 2026**, for **at least 30 days**
  - Tell selected entrants their results: by **3 December 2026**
- **Entering costs $1**, paid online when you submit your entry. Anyone asking for more to "register" you is not the US government.
- **You will need a valid passport.** New rules require your passport details and a scan of your passport.

## What is the Diversity Visa (DV) lottery?

The Diversity Visa Program, often called the "American lottery" or "Green Card lottery", gives up to **55,000 US immigrant visas (green cards)** each year to people from countries with low immigration to the United States. Winners are chosen at random by computer. If you are selected and your visa is approved, you, your spouse and your unmarried children under 21 can live and work permanently in the United States.

It is not a scholarship, but many students and young graduates in our community ask about it every year, so we follow it closely.

## What happened: the timeline

- **16 September 2025:** A new fee rule created a **$1 registration fee** for every entry. ([Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/09/16/2025-17851/schedule-of-fees-for-consular-services-department-of-state-and-overseas-embassies-and))
- **5 November 2025:** The State Department announced changes to the DV entry process and said it would announce the DV-2027 registration dates "as soon as practicable". It confirmed that DV-2027 winners can apply for visas from **1 October 2026 to 30 September 2027**. ([official notice](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/News/visas-news/changes-to-2027-dv-program-entry-period.html))
- **11 March 2026:** A final rule added **passport requirements** for entries, in force since 10 April 2026 and applied to DV-2027. ([Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/03/11/2026-04737/visas-enhancing-vetting-and-combatting-fraud-in-the-diversity-immigrant-visa-program))
- **October 2025 to October 2026:** Registration, which usually opens every October, never opened for DV-2027.
- **14 October 2026:** In the case *Red Eagle Law v. Rubio*, Judge Jinsook Ohta of the US District Court for the Southern District of California ordered the State Department to open DV-2027 registration. The same day, both sides agreed to later deadlines, which the judge approved: dates announced by 29 October, registration open by 1 November, results by 3 December 2026.

## Who can enter

You must meet **two** conditions (the rules used in recent years; check the DV-2027 instructions when they come out):

1. **Your country must be eligible.** Each year some countries are excluded because many of their nationals already moved to the US in the last five years. For DV-2026, for example, India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nigeria and China were not eligible. You may still qualify through your spouse's country of birth, or in some cases your parents' country of birth.
2. **Education or work experience:** at least a **high school education** (12 years of school, or equivalent), **or** two years of work experience in the last five years in a job that needs at least two years of training.

There is **no English test** and no age limit, but you must meet the education or work rule.

**Important:** in 2026 the US has restricted or paused immigrant visas for nationals of some countries. Winning the lottery does not guarantee a visa. Before you spend money on travel or documents, check the latest rules for your nationality on [travel.state.gov](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/immigrate/diversity-visa-program-entry.html).

## What to prepare now

- **A valid passport.** You will need its number, issuing country and expiry date, plus a **JPEG scan** (not PDF, under 5 MB) of the photo page and signature page. Stateless people and a few other cases are exempt.
- **A recent digital photo** of you, and of your spouse and each child, following the official photo rules. A wrong or old photo is one of the most common reasons entries are disqualified.
- **Your family details.** You must list your spouse and **all** your unmarried children under 21, even if they will not move with you. Forgetting one can disqualify you later.
- **A way to pay $1 online.**

## How to enter (when it opens)

1. Go to **dvprogram.state.gov** yourself. Do not use links from ads or messages.
2. Fill in the form, upload your photos and passport scan, and pay the $1 fee.
3. **Write down your confirmation number.** You need it to check your result. The US government will not email or text you to say you won.
4. Submit **only one entry**. More than one entry for the same person disqualifies all of them. Your spouse may submit a separate entry.
5. Check your result on the same website (Entrant Status Check) on the date the State Department announces.

## Avoid scams

- The US government **never** emails, texts or calls you to say you won.
- Nobody can raise your chances. Agents who promise a "guaranteed" win are lying.
- The only fee to enter is **$1**, paid on the official site.
- Official US government sites end in **.gov**. Sites like "dvlottery" with .com or .org are not official.
