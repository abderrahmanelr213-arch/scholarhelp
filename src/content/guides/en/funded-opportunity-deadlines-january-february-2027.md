## Short answer

**Many of the best funded opportunities for 2027 close in January and February 2027.** If you want to apply, start now: ask for recommendation letters, book any language test you need, and prepare your documents before the December holidays. Below is a list of funded opportunities on ScholarHelp with deadlines in this period. Each listing links to the official page.

## Which deadlines fall in late December and January?

- **31 December 2026:** [EMLE Erasmus Mundus Master in Law and Economics](/opportunities/erasmus-mundus-emle-law-economics-2027/), fully funded. IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL 95.
- **3 January 2027:** [KAUST Fellowship for MS and PhD](/opportunities/kaust-fellowship-fall-2027/), Saudi Arabia, fully funded.
- **6 January 2027:** [Eiffel Excellence Scholarship](/opportunities/eiffel-excellence-scholarship-2027/), France, fully funded. You apply through a French university, which often has an earlier internal deadline.
- **8 January 2027:** [EUROPUBHEALTH+ Master in Public Health](/opportunities/europubhealth-erasmus-mundus-public-health-2027/), fully funded. IELTS 5.5 to 6.5 depending on the track.
- **15 January 2027:** [Hansen Leadership Institute](/opportunities/hansen-leadership-institute-2027-usa/), USA, fully funded. A leadership programme for young people.
- **22 January 2027:** [Erasmus Mundus CLMCE in Children's Literature](/opportunities/erasmus-mundus-clmce-childrens-literature-2027/), fully funded.

## Which deadlines fall in February?

- **1 February 2027:** [St. Gallen Symposium Global Ideas Competition](/opportunities/st-gallen-symposium-global-ideas-competition-2027/), Switzerland, fully funded for the winners.
- **11 February 2027:** [Heidelberg Laureate Forum](/opportunities/heidelberg-laureate-forum-2027/), Germany, partially funded. For young researchers in maths and computer science.

## How should you plan?

1. **Pick the two or three that fit you best.** Quality beats quantity.
2. **Count back four to six weeks** from each deadline. That is when you should ask for letters and book tests.
3. **Use one master set of documents**, then adapt it. Our [documents checklist](/guides/scholarship-application-documents-checklist/) helps.
4. **Write a specific motivation letter for each.** See [how to write a motivation letter](/guides/how-to-write-a-motivation-letter/).
5. **Avoid last-minute mistakes.** Read [12 common application mistakes](/guides/common-scholarship-application-mistakes/).
