## About

The Yenching Academy is an interdisciplinary master's program at Peking University in Beijing. Scholars study China from many angles, such as politics, economics, history, culture and international relations. Every admitted scholar receives a full fellowship. The 2027 cohort starts in September 2027.

## Financial benefits

- Full tuition fees
- Accommodation in Beijing
- A monthly living stipend
- One round-trip flight
- Costs of field study trips

## Eligibility criteria

- International applicants of any nationality, plus applicants from Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan
- A bachelor's degree in any field, or final-year students who graduate by August 2027
- A strong academic record and a real interest in studying China
- Leadership potential and community involvement
- English proficiency, for example IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 100 (native speakers and English-taught graduates are usually exempt)

## How to apply

1. Read the admissions page and check the English test rules (tests must be taken after 1 September 2025).
2. Write a personal statement (up to 750 words) and a statement of research interest (up to 1,500 words).
3. Prepare your CV, transcripts, diploma or enrollment certificate, and ask two professors for recommendation letters.
4. Submit the online application before 30 November 2026, 9:00 am Beijing time.
