## About

The Japan Foundation is Japan's main body for international cultural exchange. Its Japanese Studies Fellowship lets scholars and doctoral candidates from abroad do research in Japan on a Japan-related topic in the humanities or social sciences. Fellowships must start between 20 June 2027 and 20 March 2028. The deadline is 1 December 2026 at 1 p.m. Japan time, and results come out by the end of April 2027.

## Financial benefits

- A round-trip discount economy air ticket to Japan
- Monthly stipend: 310,000 yen for doctoral candidates and 400,000 yen for long-term researchers
- Short-term researchers (21 to 89 days) get 17,000 yen per day instead
- Settling-in allowance of 250,000 yen (long-term only) and departure allowance of 50,000 yen
- Overseas travel insurance, plus allowances for a health certificate, dependents and enrolment fees where they apply

## Eligibility criteria

- Citizens or permanent residents of a country with diplomatic relations with Japan, living in that country
- Doctoral candidates in the humanities or social sciences who have finished, or will finish, their coursework and need 4 to 12 months in Japan for their dissertation
- Researchers with a PhD or equivalent experience, for 21 to 89 days or 4 to 12 months
- Good Japanese or English. Doctoral candidates who can work in Japanese get priority
- An advisor or research host in Japan must be arranged before the deadline
- Not for natural sciences, medicine or engineering, and not for undergraduate or master's students
- Applicants based at Southeast Asian institutions use the separate JFJSA-ASEAN Fellowship instead

## How to apply

1. Find an advisor or research associate in Japan and ask them to complete the Letter of Affiliation form.
2. Ask two referees (not your Japan host) to complete reference forms. Doctoral candidates need one from their own university.
3. Doctoral candidates also need transcripts and a Japanese-Language Ability Assessment form.
4. Submit everything on the Japan Foundation Online Application Portal (apply.jpf.go.jp) by 1 December 2026, 1 p.m. Japan time. US citizens and residents apply through the Japan Foundation New York portal instead.
