## About

The Swiss Government Excellence Scholarships are awarded by the Federal Commission for Scholarships for Foreign Students (FCS, also called ESKAS) on behalf of the Swiss Confederation. They support international exchange and research cooperation between Switzerland and more than 180 countries.

The research scholarship is for young researchers who want to do a research stay or a PhD at a Swiss university. It is partially funded: it pays a monthly stipend and some extra costs, but not tuition fees.

## Host institution / country

You do your research at a Swiss cantonal university, a university of applied sciences, ETH Zurich, EPFL or an ETH Domain research institute, under a Swiss academic supervisor who supports your application. The scholarship starts on 1 September 2027.

## Financial benefits

- CHF 2,450 per month for basic living costs (one person)
- Compulsory health insurance paid for holders from outside the EU/EFTA
- A one-time CHF 600 grant for a rental deposit
- A Half-Fare Travelcard for Swiss public transport
- A return flight allowance for holders from outside the EU/EFTA (amount set by country)
- **Not covered:** tuition and semester fees (about CHF 600 to 2,000 per semester at some universities), conference costs, fieldwork abroad and family allowances

## Eligibility criteria

- Citizens of one of the 183 eligible countries (check your country's list)
- Born after 31 December 1991
- A master's degree or equivalent completed by 31 July 2027 (30 June 2027 for ETH Zurich)
- A supervisor at a Swiss university who supports your research project
- Research fellowships give priority to PhD candidates from abroad who have not studied or researched in Switzerland before
- If you already live in Switzerland, you must have arrived on 1 August 2026 or later

## Required documents

- A full CV
- A motivation letter (maximum 2 pages)
- A research proposal on the FCS/ESKAS form (maximum 5 pages, with a summary for non-specialists)
- A letter of support from your Swiss supervisor, plus their short CV
- For research fellowships: a short CV of your home-university supervisor
- Two professors who will upload recommendation letters
- Degree certificates and transcripts (certified translations if not in English, French, Italian or German)
- A passport copy

## How to apply

1. Check that your country is eligible and find its deadline on the official country list.
2. Contact a professor at a Swiss university and get a letter of support for your project.
3. Register on the GO ESKAS online portal (open since 20 August 2026).
4. Upload all documents and give the emails of your two referees.
5. Submit online before your country's deadline. Applications by email or on paper are not accepted.

## Selection process and timeline

- Applications are judged on your academic profile, research skills and motivation, the originality and method of your project, and the quality of supervision.
- The FCS announces its decisions by the end of May 2027 at the latest.
- The scholarship starts on 1 September 2027. Research stays last 6 to 12 months with no extension; PhD scholarships are for 12 months and can be renewed up to 36 months.

## Tips for a strong application

- Start by finding a Swiss supervisor; without their letter you cannot apply.
- Write a clear 5-page proposal that shows why your project needs Switzerland.
- Budget for tuition fees, because the scholarship does not pay them.
- Applying is free. Ignore anyone who asks for money to "process" your application.
