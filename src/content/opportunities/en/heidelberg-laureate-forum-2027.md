## About

The Heidelberg Laureate Forum (HLF) is a yearly networking week in Heidelberg, Germany. It brings together winners of the top prizes in mathematics and computer science, such as the Abel Prize, the ACM Turing Award and the Fields Medal, with selected young researchers from around the world. The 14th HLF runs from 11 to 18 September 2027. Applications open on 11 November 2026 and close on 11 February 2027.

## Financial benefits

- One single hotel room with breakfast from 11 to 18 September 2027, booked and paid by the Foundation
- Lunches, dinners and coffee breaks at the venue
- Shuttle buses between Frankfurt Airport and Heidelberg on arrival and departure days
- Travel is not paid automatically. If your university or a national agency cannot fund your trip, you can apply for an HLF travel grant that covers travel and visa costs (grant window: 3 May to 23 June 2027, with a rejection letter from other funders)

## Eligibility criteria

- Students and researchers in mathematics, computer science or a closely related field
- Bachelor's or master's students who do not yet hold a master's degree
- PhD students enrolled in a doctoral programme
- Postdocs who earned their PhD within the last five years (one extra year per child born after the PhD)
- You can take part only once per academic level
- The official FAQ sets no nationality limit

## How to apply

1. Wait for the application portal to open on 11 November 2026.
2. Prepare a CV and a motivation statement in English. PhD and postdoc applicants also need a thesis summary, transcripts and any publications.
3. Ask a professor or supervisor to upload a reference letter directly in the application system.
4. Add any awards or scientific achievements, with diplomas in the original language plus English translations.
5. Submit online before 11 February 2027 at 11:59 p.m. Berlin time.
