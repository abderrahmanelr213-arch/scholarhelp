## About

The France Excellence Eiffel programme helps French universities attract top international students. The 2027 call is open for studies starting in 2027-28. Students cannot apply alone. A French university must submit your file to Campus France by 6 January 2027. Universities set earlier internal deadlines, some as early as November 2026. Results are announced in the week of 12 April 2027.

## Financial benefits

- €1,200 per month for master's students
- €2,100 per month for PhD students
- Up to 12 months for M2, 24 months for M1 and 36 months for an engineering degree
- The grant does not pay tuition fees, so check the fees at your university

## Eligibility criteria

- Non-French students (dual French nationals are not eligible)
- Master's applicants up to 29 years old and PhD applicants up to 35, as set by the programme rules
- Fields: engineering, sciences, health, digital and ecological transition, law, political science, economics, management, history and French studies
- First-time Eiffel applicants not holding another French government scholarship

## How to apply

1. Choose a master's or PhD programme at a French university in an eligible field.
2. Apply for admission and contact the university's international office about Eiffel.
3. Send your Eiffel file to the university before its internal deadline.
4. The university selects candidates and submits them to Campus France by 6 January 2027.
