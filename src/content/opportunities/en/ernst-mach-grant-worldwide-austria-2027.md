## About

The Ernst Mach Grant (worldwide) is run by OeAD, Austria's agency for education and internationalisation. It funds young researchers from all disciplines and countries to carry out a research project at an Austrian university. Stays last from 1 to 9 months and can start from September 2027. The online application opened in September and closes on 1 December 2026 at 23:59 CET. Results are expected in early July 2027.

## Financial benefits

- A monthly grant for the length of the research stay (1 to 9 full months)
- Supervision by a lecturer at an Austrian university
- The official FAQ says the grant does not cover all costs. You will need your own money for travel, health insurance and housing deposits
- The official pages do not state the monthly amount, so check the grant rules on grants.at before you apply

## Eligibility criteria

- Young researchers from any country except Austria
- No older than 35 when the stay starts
- PhD students enrolled outside Austria
- Postgraduates and postdocs who finished their studies at a non-Austrian university after 30 September 2025
- Postdocs working as lecturers or researchers at a non-Austrian university
- Not for current students in Austria, people living permanently in Austria, or people who stayed in Austria in the six months before the grant

## How to apply

1. Find a lecturer at an Austrian university who will supervise your project, and get their signed confirmation on the OeAD template.
2. Get one or two signed recommendation letters on letterhead, no older than six months.
3. Have a valid biometric passport ready.
4. Fill in every section of the online application, meeting the minimum word counts, and declare any use of AI tools.
5. Submit online before 1 December 2026, 23:59 CET. Late documents are not accepted.
