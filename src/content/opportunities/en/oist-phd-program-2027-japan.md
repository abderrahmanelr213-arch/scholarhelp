## About

OIST is an English-language graduate university in Okinawa, Japan. Its interdisciplinary PhD program covers physics, chemistry, biology, neuroscience, mathematics, computer science and engineering. OIST says the program is fully funded for every student, whatever their nationality. You can join with a bachelor's degree, without a master's.

## Financial benefits

- Full tuition
- A taxable living support of about 2.6 million yen a year, rising to about 3.6 million yen as you progress
- Subsidized on-campus apartments
- Relocation costs, including moving and travel to Japan
- A separate budget for conference and research travel

## Eligibility criteria

- Applicants of any nationality
- A bachelor's or master's degree, or expected before enrollment
- TOEFL iBT or IELTS scores if your degree was not taught in English
- Strong research interest that matches an OIST faculty member

## How to apply

1. Browse the OIST faculty list and find research units that match your interests.
2. Prepare your transcripts, CV, a statement of purpose (up to 800 words) and a passport scan.
3. Ask two to five professors for recommendation letters. At least two must arrive before you can submit.
4. Submit the online application and pay the 5,000 yen fee before 15 November 2026 (23:59 Japan time).
5. If shortlisted (late December 2026), attend the online Admissions Workshop between 18 January and 7 February 2027.
