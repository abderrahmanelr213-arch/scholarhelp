## About

Children's Literature, Media and Cultural Entrepreneurship (CLMCE) is a 24-month Erasmus Mundus Joint Master about books, films and media for children and young people, and the business of publishing and culture. Applications for September 2027 entry are open.

It suits graduates in literature, languages, media, education or history who want to work in publishing, libraries, film festivals, cultural organisations or research.

## Host institution / country

- **Semester 1:** University of Glasgow, United Kingdom
- **Semester 2:** Aarhus University, Denmark
- **Semester 3 (you choose):** Nantes, France (cultural business and marketing); Tilburg, Netherlands (literature, translation and reviewing); or Wrocław, Poland (children's film)
- **Semester 4:** a 20,000 to 25,000 word dissertation, supervised across the partners
- **Optional summer school:** University of British Columbia (Canada) or Mainz and Frankfurt (Germany), at no extra cost

Every semester 3 option includes a placement with publishers, libraries, literary institutions or a film festival. Teaching is in English. You graduate with a multiple degree from the universities on your path.

## Financial benefits

The Erasmus Mundus scholarship pays:

- All tuition fees
- Full travel insurance for the programme
- €1,400 per month for 24 months (up to €33,600 in total)

Up to 90 scholarships are offered over four editions, including up to 30 extra targeted scholarships for students from regions such as Africa, the Middle East and Asia.

## Eligibility criteria

- A first degree with a UK 2:1 or equivalent grade, in English or another modern language, literature, history, film and media, childhood studies, anthropology, sociology, library studies or arts and humanities education
- Final-year students can apply; the offer will depend on getting the degree
- Any nationality. Scholarships depend on nationality and residence, and you cannot apply if you already held an Erasmus Mundus Joint Master scholarship
- For scholarships, preference may go to applicants who do not already have a master's degree
- **Language requirement:** IELTS Academic 6.5 with no part below 6.0, or TOEFL iBT 92 (new scoring, no part below 4.5), PTE Academic 65, Duolingo 120 or another accepted test. Graduates of a recent degree from a majority English-speaking country may be exempt. These are the University of Glasgow's published requirements for this subject area.

## Required documents

- Scholarship application form (uploaded as PDF)
- Degree certificate(s) and transcripts, with official English translations
- Two reference letters (one academic; one academic or professional), dated within the last 24 months, signed, on letterhead and sent from an official email
- CV
- English test certificate (must be uploaded by the scholarship deadline)
- Passport photo page

## How to apply

1. Apply online through the University of Glasgow application portal. No other way is accepted.
2. Choose the scholarship route and upload the scholarship application form.
3. Rank your three semester 3 options from 1 to 3.
4. Upload all documents. You have 42 days from starting the application to submit it.
5. Submit before **22 January 2027, 13:00 UK time**.

## Selection process and timeline

- Scholarship results are announced in April 2027.
- Self-funded applications are decided on a rolling basis.
- Studies start in September 2027.

## Tips for a strong application

- Ask your referees early: letters must come from official email addresses.
- Show your interest in children's books, film or culture with real examples (writing, reading clubs, teaching, volunteering).
- Explain which semester 3 path you want and why.
- Take your English test early so the certificate is ready before January.
