## About

The National Scholarship Programme of the Slovak Republic pays for short study, research and teaching stays at Slovak universities and research institutes. It does not fund full degrees. This call is for stays in the summer semester of the 2026/2027 academic year. Students start between 1 February and 1 April 2027. PhD students, teachers, researchers and artists start between 1 February and 31 August 2027. Online applications close on 31 October 2026 at 16:00 CET.

## Financial benefits

- A monthly scholarship: 620 EUR for students, 1,025.50 EUR for PhD students
- 1,025.50 to 1,470 EUR per month for university teachers, researchers and artists, depending on degree and experience
- A one-time travel grant for students and PhD students, based on distance from home (for example 1,100 EUR for 5,000 to 7,000 km, and 1,500 EUR for over 7,000 km), paid at the end of the stay
- Reimbursement of the required medical check for a residence permit, up to 250 EUR
- Student stays last one or two semesters. PhD and researcher stays last 1 to 10 months

## Eligibility criteria

- Citizens of any country in the world except Slovakia
- Students: enrolled at a university outside Slovakia, at master's level or with at least 2.5 years of study completed, and accepted by a Slovak university
- PhD students: enrolled in doctoral studies outside Slovakia and accepted by a Slovak university or research institute
- Teachers, researchers and artists: invited by an eligible Slovak institution
- Not for people seeking a full degree in Slovakia, or already funded by Erasmus+ or CEEPUS for the same stay
- Not for people who spent 15 months or more in Slovakia in the last 36 months

## How to apply

1. Find a host university or research institute in Slovakia and get an acceptance or invitation letter that states your exact dates.
2. Prepare your CV, motivation letter, study or research plan, enrolment confirmation, diplomas and recommendation letters (two from teachers for students, one from your supervisor for PhD students).
3. Submit the online application on scholarships.sk in English or Slovak before 31 October 2026, 16:00 CET. Ask for the travel grant in the same application.
4. Send the original acceptance letter so it reaches SAIA in Bratislava by 12:00 on the third working day after the deadline.
