## About

The Excellence Scholarship and Opportunity Programme (ESOP) supports outstanding students who want to do a master's degree at ETH Zurich. About 60 scholarships are expected for 2027-28. The application window for autumn 2027 entry runs from 1 to 30 November 2026, together with the master's application.

## Financial benefits

- Full tuition fee waiver
- CHF 13,500 per semester for living and study costs (from autumn 2027)
- Support for the full regular length of the master's (three or four semesters)

## Eligibility criteria

- Excellent bachelor's students, usually in the top 10% of their programme
- Applying to an ETH master's in the first application window
- Not already enrolled in an ETH master's and not already holding a master's degree
- No nationality restriction is listed

## How to apply

1. Apply for your master's programme through ETH's eApply portal between 1 and 30 November 2026.
2. Upload the normal admission documents plus a short pre-proposal for your master's thesis.
3. Name two professors as referees if your programme does not already ask for references.
4. Stay reachable for a possible phone or video interview in February. Results come at the end of March.
