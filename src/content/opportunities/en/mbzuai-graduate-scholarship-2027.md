## About

MBZUAI is a graduate research university in Abu Dhabi focused only on artificial intelligence. It offers M.Sc. and Ph.D. programs in fields such as machine learning, computer vision, natural language processing and robotics. Funded students study for free and receive a monthly stipend. The application portal for the 2027 intake is open now.

## Financial benefits

- Free tuition
- A monthly stipend
- One return flight home each year
- Health insurance
- UAE residence visa and Emirates ID
- Accommodation for Ph.D. students, according to the university's Ph.D. page

## Eligibility criteria

- Applicants of any nationality
- A STEM degree, such as computer science, engineering, mathematics or physics
- A minimum CGPA of 3.0 or 3.5 out of 4.0, depending on the program
- Final-year students can apply with their current transcript
- Note: funding is not guaranteed for every M.Sc. student, and the fee-based Master in Applied AI is not funded

## How to apply

1. Pick one program (only one application per cycle is processed) and a preferred faculty supervisor.
2. Create an account on the MBZUAI application portal and take the online screening exam.
3. Upload your CV, transcripts, statement of purpose, English proof, passport copy and photo. Ph.D. applicants also add a research statement and three referees (two for M.Sc.).
4. Submit before 15 December 2026, 5 pm UAE time. Some applicants are invited to a technical interview, and decisions come by 31 March 2027.
