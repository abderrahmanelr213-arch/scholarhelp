## About

The St. Gallen Symposium is a yearly meeting in Switzerland where young people and senior leaders from business, politics and science debate big questions. The Global Ideas Competition, formerly the Global Essay Competition, is the main way for students to get a place. The 2027 question is "What could we solve, if we could agree?" You pick a solvable problem that is stuck because people cannot agree, and propose a new arrangement to fix it. The top 100 entries are invited to the 56th Symposium in St. Gallen in late April 2027 as "Leaders of Tomorrow".

## Financial benefits

- Free participation in the Symposium for the top 100 entries
- Flights or train tickets to Switzerland, booked by the organisers
- Airport shuttle and a free public transport ticket in St. Gallen
- Board and lodging in private student flats during the Symposium
- A CHF 20,000 prize fund shared by the three best ideas
- Not covered: visa fees (an invitation letter is provided) and any guests

## Eligibility criteria

- Students of any nationality (the official page sets no nationality limit)
- Enrolled in a master's or higher programme at a research university that offers at least one PhD programme
- Still enrolled on 1 February 2027, in any field of study
- Born in 1997 or later
- Bachelor students are not eligible unless their university confirms in writing that their year equals graduate level
- Individual entries only, no teams

## How to apply

1. Check that you meet the eligibility rules on the official page.
2. Write a Solution Brief of up to 1,000 words in English using the official template. Keep your name out of the file, since the first round is anonymous.
3. Record a video of up to three minutes explaining your idea, without slides.
4. Prepare a passport or national ID and a proof of enrolment dated for 1 February 2027.
5. Fill in the online form with a 200 to 300 word abstract, declare any AI tools you used, and submit through the official portal before 1 February 2027, 11:59 p.m. (UTC-12).
6. Results are sent by email by mid-March 2027.
