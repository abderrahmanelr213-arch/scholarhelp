## About

The Clarendon Fund is one of the largest graduate scholarship schemes at the University of Oxford. Awards are based on academic merit and potential. There is no separate application form. Everyone who applies to an eligible course by its December or January deadline is considered automatically. For 2027-28 entry, course deadlines include 2 December 2026 and 6 January 2027, so check your own course page.

## Financial benefits

- Full course fees
- A grant for living expenses
- Funding lasts for the full period of fee liability of your course

## Eligibility criteria

- Students of any nationality and any country of residence
- Applying to a full-time or part-time DPhil or master's course at Oxford
- Strong academic record and potential in any field of study

## How to apply

1. Choose your Oxford graduate course and find its 2027-28 deadline on the course page.
2. Submit your graduate application with all documents and references by the December or January deadline.
3. You are considered for Clarendon automatically, with no extra form.
4. Wait for decisions, which are usually made between late February and June 2027.
