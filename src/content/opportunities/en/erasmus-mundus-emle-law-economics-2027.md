## About

The European Master in Law and Economics (EMLE) is an Erasmus Mundus Joint Master. Students study the economic analysis of law at partner universities in different European countries. Applications for the 2027-28 intake opened on 1 September 2026. All admitted applicants are considered for Erasmus Mundus scholarships, so there is no separate scholarship form.

## Financial benefits

- A monthly contribution of up to €1,400 for travel, visa, settling in and living costs
- Under Erasmus+ rules, Erasmus Mundus scholarships also cover the programme's participation costs
- Study in more than one European country

## Eligibility criteria

- At least 240 ECTS credits (or equivalent) of previous higher education
- Law or economics background preferred; other degrees accepted with a strong motivation letter
- Final-year students who will graduate before July 2027
- Open to applicants from all countries

## How to apply

1. Create an account at apply.emle.org.
2. Upload your degree certificates, transcripts and a one-page motivation letter.
3. Request one or two recommendation letters through the online system.
4. Upload proof of residence dated within the last 12 months (a passport is not accepted).
5. Submit by 31 December 2026 if you are non-European, or 15 January 2027 if you are European.
