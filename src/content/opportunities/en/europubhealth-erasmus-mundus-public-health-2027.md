## About

EUROPUBHEALTH+ is a two-year Erasmus Mundus Joint Master in public health. The European Commission has recognised it as a Master of Excellence since 2006. Students live and study in at least two European countries and graduate with a double master's degree, one from each university on their study path.

The programme mixes health policy, epidemiology, data and management. It suits graduates from any field who want a career in public health, health services or international health organisations.

## Host institution / country

**Year 1 (foundation course):** University College Dublin (Ireland), University of Granada (Spain), University of Liège (Belgium) or University of Sheffield (United Kingdom).

**Year 2 (specialisation):**

- Rennes, France (EHESP): advanced biostatistics and epidemiology; health and environment in a changing climate; health promotion and prevention
- Bordeaux, France: public health data science
- Granada, Spain: health services management and digital transformation
- Maastricht, Netherlands: leadership in European public health
- Kraków, Poland: governance and economics of health systems

Teaching is in English, with some tracks in Spanish or French.

## Financial benefits

The Erasmus Mundus scholarship includes:

- Up to €1,400 per month for living costs
- Full coverage of the participation (tuition) fees
- Health and accident insurance for the whole programme

Scholarships are only given in the January round, and their number is limited. Applicants who are admitted without a scholarship can ask for a fee waiver of up to 30%.

## Eligibility criteria

- A bachelor's degree of at least 180 ECTS (or an equivalent) in any subject
- Open to all nationalities
- You cannot apply if you already received an Erasmus Mundus Joint Master scholarship
- **Language requirement:**
    - English, depending on your study track: IELTS 6.5 (no part below 6.0) for Dublin or Sheffield in year 1; IELTS 6.0 (no part below 5.5) for Liège or Granada with most year-2 options; IELTS 5.5 (no part below 5.0) for Liège or Granada with health promotion in year 2
    - TOEFL iBT, PTE Academic and Cambridge tests are also accepted; Duolingo only for the IELTS 5.5 track
    - Spanish at B2 (DELE or SIELE) for the Granada tracks, or French at B2 (DELF, DALF, TCF or TEF) for the Liège and Rennes health promotion tracks
    - Some citizens of English-speaking countries and holders of degrees taught fully in English are exempt

## Required documents

- Online application form in English
- Bachelor's degree certificate and transcripts
- Proof of language level (test certificate or exemption)
- Other supporting documents listed in the applicant instructions on the official site

Book your language test early. The official site says an application without language proof cannot be reviewed.

## How to apply

1. Read the admission requirements and the applicant instructions on the official site.
2. Choose your study track (year 1 and year 2 cities), since it decides your language requirement.
3. Create an account on the official application platform and upload all documents.
4. Submit before **8 January 2027, 1:00 pm CET** to be considered for a scholarship.

## Selection process and timeline

- Applications are checked for eligibility, then scored on motivation, academic record, experience in public health or social work, international projects and personal skills.
- Top-ranked scholarship applicants have a 15-minute online interview on **18 February 2027**.
- Scholarship results are released in mid-March 2027.
- Self-funded round: deadline 24 March 2027, results in mid-May 2027.

## Tips for a strong application

- Pick your track first, then take the right language test for it.
- Explain clearly what public health problem in your country you want to work on.
- Show any volunteering or work in health, NGOs or community projects.
- Submit well before the deadline, especially if you need a visa.
