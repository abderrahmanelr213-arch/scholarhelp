## About

Gates Cambridge funds outstanding students from outside the UK to study at the University of Cambridge. It looks for strong academics who also want to improve the lives of others. Applications for entry in 2027-28 opened on 11 September 2026. For most applicants the deadline is 8 December 2026 or 6 January 2027, depending on the course. US citizens living in the USA had an earlier deadline of 14 October 2026.

## Financial benefits

- Full University Composition Fee
- A maintenance allowance (£23,152 for 12 months at the 2026-27 rate)
- One economy flight at the start and end of the course
- Inbound visa costs and the Immigration Health Surcharge
- Possible extra support for academic development, children and fieldwork

## Eligibility criteria

- Citizen of any country other than the United Kingdom
- Applying for a full-time PhD, full-time MLitt or a full-time one-year postgraduate course at Cambridge
- MBA, MASt and some professional degrees are not eligible
- Strong academic record, leadership potential and commitment to improving the lives of others

## How to apply

1. Choose your Cambridge course and check its "Course Funding Deadline" in the University Course Directory.
2. Apply through the University's Graduate Application Portal.
3. Complete the Gates Cambridge part of the funding section and answer the four scholarship questions.
4. Add a research proposal if you are applying for a PhD.
5. Ask two academic referees and one Gates Cambridge referee to submit before your deadline.
