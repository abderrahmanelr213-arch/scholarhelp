## About

KAUST is a graduate research university on the Red Sea coast of Saudi Arabia. It offers MS, MS/PhD and PhD programs in science and engineering. Admitted students automatically receive the KAUST Fellowship, so there is no separate scholarship form. The Round 2 window for Fall 2027 entry is open now.

## Financial benefits

- Full tuition fees
- Free on-campus accommodation
- Health insurance
- Relocation support
- A living stipend (KAUST says it is more than the average student spending of about USD 1,320 a month)

## Eligibility criteria

- Applicants of any nationality
- A bachelor's degree (for MS) or relevant degree (for PhD) with a GPA of at least 3.0 out of 4.0 or equivalent
- TOEFL or IELTS scores, unless your degree is from the US, Canada, UK, Ireland, Australia or New Zealand
- Students already funded by an outside sponsor do not get the fellowship on top of their sponsorship

## How to apply

1. Choose your program and check the entry requirements on the KAUST admissions site.
2. Create an account on the KAUST application portal. There is no application fee.
3. Upload transcripts, your statement and English test scores, and add your referees.
4. Submit before 3 January 2027. Decisions are released around 14 February 2027 and studies start on 29 August 2027.
