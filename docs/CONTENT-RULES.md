# Content and design rules

Rules Abdou set for ScholarHelp. Follow them for every new listing, page and design change.

## Listings

- **Real and verified only.** Every fact (amounts, dates, eligibility, documents) comes from the official site of the organisation. Never guess a GPA, fee, word count, test score or number of references.
- **Own words.** Summaries and descriptions are written in our own words, never copied. Copied text ranks badly on Google and is refused by AdSense.
- **Both official links, always.** In the Quick overview:
  - "Offered by" links to the organisation's official homepage (`hostUrl`).
  - "Opportunity" links to the official announcement of this year's call (`officialUrl`, or `link` if empty).
- **Apply link.** The orange **Apply here** button at the bottom of the page goes to where students apply (`link`).
- **Full explanation first.** The page reads: summary, full description, then the Quick overview, then **Apply here**.
- **Description headings, in order:** About, Host institution / country, Financial benefits, Eligibility criteria, Required documents, How to apply, Selection process and timeline, Tips for a strong application.
- **Funding.** Only call something "Fully funded" when the official site confirms it. Otherwise use Partially funded or Travel grant.
- **Two deadlines.** Put the main one in Deadline and the other in the deadline note.

## Keeping visitors on the site

- The card's **Apply Now** opens our own opportunity page, not the official site.
- The only ways out to the official site are the orange **Apply here** button and the two official links in the Quick overview.
- The registration pop-up (name, email, phone with country code) keeps coming back until the visitor registers or joins the Telegram group.
- The official links (**Apply here** and the two Quick overview links) only open after the visitor fills in the registration form. Joining Telegram stops the pop-ups but does not unlock them.
- A visitor who registered once is never asked again on that phone or browser (saved in the browser and a 5-year cookie). On a new device they tap **Already registered? Continue with your email** and only type their email.

## Look and feel

- **Brand colours:** navy `#0C2B4C` and teal `#0F8D8E`. Register button and "Fully funded" tag are red.
- **Flags:** always show the country flag beside a country or location name.
- **Readable text:** dark, at least 17px, weight 500 or more. Menu links are dark navy and bold.
- **Real brand icons** for Telegram, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and TikTok.
- **Card picture:** announcement style. The opportunity name in large dark text, the country flag, our small logo and "scholarhelp.com". No days-left counter. A "Deadline is close" badge appears in the last 14 days.
- **Phones first:** most visitors use phones. Opportunities must appear on the first screen of the home page.

## Social media posts

- A simple picture: the university or organisation logo (or the country flag), **one** short phrase and the ScholarHelp logo.
- All details go in the caption, with the link to our page.
- Posts are only made when Abdou asks.

## Languages

English (default), Arabic and Pashto. Arabic and Pashto pages read right to left. New interface text needs all three languages. Pashto text should be checked by a native speaker.
